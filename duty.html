FUNDAMENTAL DUTIES

RESPECT FOR THE CONSTITUTION

It is the duty of every citizen to respect the Constitution, its ideals, institutions, and the rule of law. The Constitution is the foundation of democracy and ensures justice, liberty, equality, and fraternity for all citizens.

RESPECT FOR THE NATAIONAL FLAG AND NATAIONAL ANTHEM

Citizens must honor the National Flag and the National Anthem. They represent the pride, unity, and sovereignty of the nation and remind people of the sacrifices made for independence.

FOLLOW THE IDEALS OF THE FREEDOM STRUGGLE

Every citizen should remember and follow the noble ideals of the freedom struggle, such as truth, non-violence, and patriotism, which guided India to independence.

PROTECT UNITY AND INTERGRITY OF INDIA

Citizens must safeguard the unity, sovereignty, and integrity of India. Any activity that threatens national unity should be strongly opposed.

DEFEND THE NATION

It is the duty of citizens to defend the country and render national service whenever required. National security depends on the collective responsibility of its people.

PROMOTE HARMONY AND BROTHERHOOD

Citizens should promote harmony and brotherhood among all people of India, rising above differences of religion, language, region, or caste.

PRESERVE CULTURAL HERITAGE

India has a rich cultural heritage. Citizens must protect and preserve monuments, traditions, art, and cultural values passed down through generations.

PROTECT THE ENVIRONMENT

Every citizen should protect the natural environment, including forests, rivers, wildlife, and natural resources, and show compassion for all living beings.

DEVELOP SCIENTIFIC TEMPER

Citizens should develop scientific temper, humanism, and a spirit of inquiry and reform to encourage rational thinking and progress in society.

SAFEGUARD PUBLIC PROPERTY

It is the duty of citizens to protect public property and avoid violence. Public property belongs to everyone and should be used responsibly.

PROVIDE EDUCATION TO CHILDREN

Parents or guardians must provide opportunities for education to children between the ages of 6 and 14 years, ensuring their proper growth and future.
