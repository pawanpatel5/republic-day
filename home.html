ARCHITECTS OF THE INDIAN CONSTITUTION

Dr. B. R. Ambedkar was born on April 14, 1891. He was a great social reformer, jurist, and the principal architect of the Indian Constitution. He also did a lot for the rights of Dalits in India and was honored with the ‘Bharat Ratna’ award. He was a strong advocate for education, equality, and social justice.
